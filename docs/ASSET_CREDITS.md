# Asset Credits & Licensing Notes

This portfolio repository contains screenshots, recorded gameplay and documentation; it intentionally excludes importable third-party art packs and the complete Unreal project.

- **RealisticDesertPack (Fab)**: desert environment textures, rocks and vegetation. Creator, listing and licensing details still need verification.
- **Off-road vehicle and other assets**: third-party art used in the prototype; original creators retain authorship.
- **`cc0_arrow_5`**: route guidance asset. The actual listing and license must be verified rather than inferred from its name.
- **Epic Games / Unreal Engine 5**: game engine, Blueprints, PCG, Landscape and Niagara.

The portfolio author worked on gameplay integration, level and route design, PCG placement, interactions and UI rather than original creation of third-party models. Check individual asset terms before distributing a packaged game.
