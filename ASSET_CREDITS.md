# Third-Party Content and Attribution

This portfolio contains **screenshots and documentation only**. It does not redistribute imported Fab meshes, textures, animation files, or source asset packs.

## Content sources used during development

- **Fab / RealisticDesertPack:** desert terrain/environment assets, plants and rocks (publisher and individual listing URL to be confirmed).
- **Fab / other imported assets:** vehicle meshes, environment props, and arrow models (exact creator, asset listing and license to be confirmed against the user’s Fab library).
- **Unreal Engine / Epic Games:** engine-provided components, Blueprint, PCG, Landscape and Niagara tools.

## Before adding any files or distributing a build

1. Identify each imported pack and save its Fab listing URL, author, and license.
2. Verify whether attribution is required, especially for Creative Commons (e.g. CC BY).
3. Do **not** upload original reusable third-party `.uasset`, `.fbx`, textures, or other asset packages to the public repository without permission under their license.
4. For collaboration or redistribution of a playable build, recheck the applicable license terms and sharing requirements.

This document is a transparency note, **not** a substitute for the asset license terms.
