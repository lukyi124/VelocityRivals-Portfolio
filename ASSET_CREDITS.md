# Asset Credits & Licensing Notes

This repository publicly distributes **only** screenshots, a recorded gameplay video and documentation. It does **not** redistribute importable third-party meshes, textures, Fab packs, or the complete Unreal project.

## Third-party components

- **RealisticDesertPack (Fab):** desert Landscape textures, vegetation, rocks, scenery. Original creator, Fab listing URL and license details need final verification against the asset's listing in the user's Fab library.
- **Off-road vehicle / environment art (Fab and Unreal sample content):** where used, the original artists retain authorship. Exact listings and license terms should be recorded before sharing a full build or raw project assets.
- **Directional arrow `cc0_arrow_5` (Fab):** used for route guidance. Despite the asset name, verify its actual listing and license before making a licensing claim.
- **Unreal Engine 5 / Epic Games:** editor, engine systems, Blueprint, PCG, Landscape and Niagara tools.

The project author contributed the level design, route design, gameplay and effect setup, Blueprint scripting, PCG configuration and asset integration, **not** the original 3D creation of those art packs.

## Publication boundaries

- The MP4 contains only recorded gameplay; it is not a reusable source asset pack.
- Source `.uasset`, `.fbx`, textures and third-party content remain outside the public showcase.
- Before any publicly distributable packaged game, audit licenses, creator attribution requirements, and whether the particular content may be distributed in a build.

If you are a listed artist and would like attribution completed or corrected, please contact the repository owner.
